# Redirect

For the Chief. Send the current Coordinator one concise update with what
changed, the evidence or reason, and the next action. Explicitly supersede any
conflicting instruction. Keep the same assignment, owner, authority, and
original acceptance when the goal is unchanged; the Coordinator may revise
worker ownership within that assignment. Do not create a duplicate Coordinator
for an ordinary method change.

Change ownership only when the assignment outcome or a consequential
coordination boundary changes. Preserve failures and prior evidence, then
require affected verification and `/prove-it` on the integrated revision.
